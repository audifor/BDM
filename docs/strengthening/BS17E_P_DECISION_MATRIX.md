## Existing Finance reuse and implementation boundary

Reuse canonical Finance authority for organization currency/profile, authorization and reservation, commitment, recognition, payable, Treasury settlement, ledger, recurring sources, and Calendar. Reuse Governance decision types, grants, rights, and event lifecycle. Reuse CFI7 as an integration seam and Facilities identifiers as physical provenance. Do not add a Facilities budget, cash balance, price calculator, second ledger, payment scheduler, or alternate approval model.

Potential adapters requiring approved scope: Facilities source ↔ typed Governance decision; pre-work Finance reservation and binding commitment lifecycle; Facility provenance links to reservation/payable/settlement; recurring service source if an approved contract exists; Save V4 validation of these references. This is a list of likely gaps, not authorization to change schemas.

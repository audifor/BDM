# BS17 Facilities Strengthening Roadmap

This roadmap is derived from [the BS17A audit](BS17A_FACILITIES_SYSTEM_TRUTH_AUDIT.md) at canonical main `8a0b359e9ae48fe372668241788c9d5aa7a33b03`. It strengthens existing CFI1-CFI8/CFI2S/CFI6A/MX0; it does not replace them. BS17A implements none of these milestones.

All future behavior below is **PROPOSED scope**, not newly DECIDED policy. Missing physical initialization, effect, closure, economic, institutional or AI rules must first be grounded in approved source-of-truth decisions. Existing factories/fixtures are not authorization to choose product rules. Work on one coherent milestone per run with the BS/MX Fast Loop, narrow focused proofs and a concrete PASS gate; do not split into cosmetic file milestones.

Dependency labels:

- **SAFE_BEFORE_BS16_CLOSES:** independent physical/query/presentation work; no Finance/Governance production writes or fabricated money permissions.
- **REQUIRES_BS16_COMPLETE:** consumes completed Finance authority and validated economic policy. All unresolved economic links are **DEFERRED_INTEGRATION_AFTER_BS16**.

## BS17B - Physical asset provisioning and one truthful Facilities surface

**CATEGORY:** SAFE_BEFORE_BS16_CLOSES

**NAME:** Canonical physical existence and career reachability.

**OBJECTIVE:** Make existing Facilities source truth reachable in actual careers from legitimate approved physical content, reconcile source location/venue references, and route manager infrastructure views to the canonical NG model. Remove or truthfully retire the reachable PCB local upgrade/bonus simulation through a narrow approved migration. Fix physical creation fidelity so non-training facilities are not blindly labeled TRAINING and sponsoring ownership/usage is never implicitly fabricated.

**CANONICAL AUTHORITY:** Existing Facility, Place, component anatomy, ownership/control/operator/usage and source-backed content/bootstrap. One app read model. BS15 owns Talent supply; physical Place shared with accepted Talent integration only through its canonical base.

**SYSTEMS TOUCHED:** Facilities domain/queries as narrowly needed, new-game/WorldGenerator/World DB physical bootstrap, Save compatibility, club/NG routing and canonical read model. No Finance balances, budgets, profiles, ledger, commitments or Governance changes.

**DEPENDENCIES:** Approved source/content provisioning and missing-data policy. Distinguish unknown ownership share/condition/standard/capacity from absent asset. No arbitrary perfect condition, asset ownership or building catalog economics. Rebase/integrate only under the later milestone's explicit authorized workflow; this roadmap grants no merge permission. BS15 ancestry must be resolved by its canonical integration process before coupling shared Talent physical facts.

**GAMEPLAY CONSEQUENCE:** Real user and AI clubs have inspectable physical infrastructure where approved content exists; honest empty/unknown state elsewhere. Manager sees one authoritative Facilities surface rather than fictional PCB levels and local money.

**FOCUSED TESTS:** Shipped prototype/ACB/World DB bootstrap truth; NBA/WNBA/shared-university content scenarios; unknown factual inputs; source identity stability; per-type new Facility creation; duplicate/save round-trip; PCB routing and no local upgrade authority; no unintended financial artifacts or Player creation.

**PASS GATE:** Source-backed canonical physical inventory is reachable from production creation paths in the explicitly approved world scope; unsupported worlds are labeled accurately. Existing identities/history/access semantics survive Save/load. UI has one physical authority and no advertised fake sporting/economic bonuses. No Finance/Governance production diff.

## BS17C - Physical operations, work progression and availability truth

**CATEGORY:** SAFE_BEFORE_BS16_CLOSES

**NAME:** Condition, commissioning, capacity facts and project disruption foundation.

**OBJECTIVE:** Close physical-engine fidelity gaps before spending or sporting policy consumes them: commissioning facts, measured-use seam where a source actually exists, wear/documentation consistency, scoped work progress/availability and coherent lifecycle restoration. Exercise multi-replacement IDs, same-day/overlap/retry behavior. Supply capacity units and limitations faithfully; do not call a display count an enforced sporting throughput constraint.

**CANONICAL AUTHORITY:** CFI4 condition/serviceability, CFI5 deterioration/repair application, CFI6 project graph/scope, CFI6A restoration. Derive readiness/capacity/work progress from approved source facts; persist only genuine work/condition history needed for authority.

**SYSTEMS TOUCHED:** Facilities domain/engine, Facilities Calendar phase, read model and NG physical inspection, Save for necessary source-state additions. Existing physical-only engine seams remain independent of financial permission; they must not be promoted to unauthorized live spend commands.

**DEPENDENCIES:** BS17B for real content; approved commissioning/wear/work-duration/disruption policy, including unknown-state handling and overlapping work. Fix demonstrable identity collisions without choosing new sporting/economic rules. Purpose-agnostic access is existing design and must not be silently changed. No economic maintenance commissioning or cost generation.

**GAMEPLAY CONSEQUENCE:** Manager can understand actual physical condition, capacity units, work status and scoped availability; assets no longer appear healthy merely because commissioning facts are absent. Project progress/history is factual and premature completion cannot be presented as finished physical work under the approved model.

**FOCUSED TESTS:** Known/unknown condition; calendar cadence versus elapsed batching; no-op/replay/same-day repair and deterioration; source load absent/present; project early completion, pause/resume, prior closures and reload; multi-component replacement; concurrent projects; component/facility scoped outages; no Finance collections changed.

**PASS GATE:** Approved physical lifecycle remains deterministic, source-traceable and Save-safe; no duplicate identities, contradictory work/status history or unapproved closure fallback. Model and comments agree. Availability/capacity facts are exposed honestly. No Finance/Governance write authority is introduced and no sporting effect is invented.

**OBJECTIVE:** Wire the existing CFI8 context into the canonical consumers for the approved capability/availability/throughput consequences. Training owns training execution/development; Medical owns diagnosis/treatment/rehab/RTP/recovery; accepted BS15 owns Talent intake/materialization/pathway. Preserve those authorities and avoid global quality bonuses.

**CANONICAL AUTHORITY:** CFI8 physical context plus Training/Medical/Development/BS15 outcome authorities. Facility standard is a physical descriptor, not a new player/coach rating. Demand/capacity allocation and effect rules require explicit approved policy.

**SYSTEMS TOUCHED:** facilitiesSporting integration, Training scheduling/execution/planning, Injury/Medical/Recovery, existing Talent consumer boundary after BS15 is on canonical base; app read models linking evidence and actions. No Finance/Governance production changes.

**DEPENDENCIES:** BS17B/C; approved sporting availability/capacity/effect and missing-facility behavior. Accepted BS15 integrated on the actual implementation base before Talent work. Medical/Training permission and diagnostic rules remain their existing authorities. No arena revenue/ticket/cash implementation in this milestone.

**GAMEPLAY CONSEQUENCE:** A demonstrated physical restriction changes an approved sporting operation or outcome and gives the manager an attributable remedy. Restored capability restores that operation. Shared team access is respected. Facility capacity may constrain approved academy usage without creating Players independently.

**FOCUSED TESTS:** Paired worlds differing only in physical access/serviceability/capacity; full/limited/out-of-service and unknown inputs; actual simultaneous demand; shared men/women/team/org rights contract; individual/team sessions; diagnosis/rehab/RTP/recovery consumers as approved; BS15 single player-supply authority; deterministic reload; no direct Match stat multiplier or hidden bonus duplication.

**PASS GATE:** Every advertised consequence is proven Facility -> canonical property -> query -> actual production consumer -> sporting result. At least the explicitly scoped core operations are live, with unsupported effects labeled absent. No new Talent generator, player mutation authority or unexplained sporting regression. No Finance/Governance production diff.

## BS17E - Authorized maintenance and development economy

**CATEGORY:** REQUIRES_BS16_COMPLETE

**NAME:** One institutional and financial decision/execution boundary.

**OBJECTIVE:** Implement real manager maintenance/project proposal and execution paths that consume approved Governance rights and completed BS16 financial permission/reservations, then materialize the correct commitment/recognition/payable/settlement/ledger chain. Separate physical progress, budget authorization, secured funding, recognized expense and paid cash. Reconcile CFI7 names/bindings/allocations instead of creating a parallel economic lifecycle.

**CANONICAL AUTHORITY:** Facilities needs/actions/projects for physical intent; Governance bodies/grants/decision events for institutional permission; completed Finance authority, approved category budget, reservation, economic-source adapter, payable/treasury/ledger for money. Debt remains Finance-owned. Physical completion never implies payment.

**SYSTEMS TOUCHED:** Facilities app commands/CFI7/read model/NG manager actions; narrowly authorized Governance consumer bridge and Finance-owned adapters/reporting integration; Save source links. Finance-owned changes require the BS16 complete authority and milestone approval, never a BS17 interim workaround.

**DEPENDENCIES:** BS17B/C; **BS16 complete**, including legitimate profile/currency/account/budget-category/period/renewal policy and reservation/settlement contracts. Approved maintenance versus CAPEX treatment, cost/duration inputs, cancellation/sunk cost, Board/action/threshold rules and recurrent OPEX source authority. Sporting relevance can consume BS17D evidence without duplicating it.

**GAMEPLAY CONSEQUENCE:** Manager can discover a need, choose approved repair/project action, obtain the actual required authorization, see actionable budget/cash/Governance blockers, execute work and trace every obligation and cash payment back to physical cause. Rejection prevents execution; cancellation preserves history and reconciles only legally releasable amounts.

**FOCUSED TESTS:** Need is not expense; approval/rejection/wrong actor; profile/currency/category/period missing; authorization versus cash; reserve/retry/consume/release once; insufficient funding; maintenance recognition and OPEX attribution; project commitment allocation; payable creation and partial/multiple settlements; recognition != payment; cancellation/reload; financial-failure atomicity; same user/AI gates.

**PASS GATE:** Source -> permission -> obligation -> recognition -> payable -> actual settlement -> ledger is traceable and reconciled under the completed Finance contract. No CFI7 'payment' outcome without cash settlement. Missing policy/input is surfaced, never invented. Sporting/physical completion and money settlement remain independent, and no second finance balance exists.

## BS17F - Organization management, manager information and horizon closure

**CATEGORY:** REQUIRES_BS16_COMPLETE

**NAME:** Autonomous Facilities decisions and fully manifested lifecycle.

**OBJECTIVE:** Add bounded Organization AI using legitimate needs, usage/shortage and sporting evidence; compare approved repair/investment choices and execute through the same BS17E gates as the manager. Close the event/attention/workspace/action/result loop and certify supported long-term wear, repairs, renovations, capacity evolution/replacement and financial history.

**CANONICAL AUTHORITY:** Existing physical and sporting facts, approved Organization knowledge/policy, BS17E shared commands, existing breakpoint/inbox/attention authority. No AI-only free projects, omniscient sporting facts, invented money or automatic Talent supply.

**SYSTEMS TOUCHED:** Narrow Facilities AI planner/executor, Calendar scheduling seam, manager attention/breakpoint routes, NG explanation/history and focused certification. Existing Finance/Governance consumers only through the settled boundaries.

**DEPENDENCIES:** BS17B-E; approved AI prioritization/knowledge/investment/obsolescence rules. Long-horizon scope must follow the available canonical worlds and settled financial renewal policy. Unsupported product decisions remain explicit; the milestone cannot silently decide them.

**GAMEPLAY CONSEQUENCE:** AI and user organizations face the same physical shortages, permissions and costs. Meaningful deterioration, need, outage, approval/blocker, delay and completion draws attention to an exact remedy. Manager can explain why an asset changed and the sporting/financial consequence across seasons.

**FOCUSED TESTS:** Legitimate knowledge; weak/unknown assets; demand shortage; repair versus replacement under approved policy; prioritization under tight authorization/liquidity; rejected/unfunded/pause/cancel routes; deterministic choices/save reload; no repeat-spend or duplicate proposal; event -> attention -> exact workspace/action -> durable result; compact multi-cycle history then approved BS17 horizon certification.

**PASS GATE:** Supported worlds demonstrate real decisions, physical work, consequence and traceable economic/history completion under shared authority. All advertised manager truths are VISIBLE/ALERTED/ACTIONABLE/CONSEQUENTIAL/NAVIGABLE where applicable. Horizon evidence proves natural renewal rather than fixture-only commands. No replay/reopening of the passed BS15 performance program unless a genuinely new scaling defect is demonstrated.

## Safe work and deferred boundary

BS17B/C/D are eligible before BS16 closes **only within their physical/query/sporting scope and after their explicit product/lineage dependencies**. 'Safe' is not permission to invent initialization, work duration, sporting multipliers or Talent policy. Preparatory financial source/binding **reads** are READY_WITH_CURRENT_FINANCE; economic commands, spending checks, recurring costs and settlement are DEFERRED_INTEGRATION_AFTER_BS16.

BS17E/F require BS16 complete. Governance gates are implemented in E with the completed spending boundary; AI then uses the same approved commands in F. Do not add a temporary budget check, local cash balance, fictional currency/account, hardcoded CAPEX authorization or separate Board approval model while waiting.

## Facilities operations × authorities

Legend: `RC` = `REUSABLE_CERTIFIED`; `NA` = `NEEDS_ADAPTER`; `PP` = `REQUIRES_PRODUCT_POLICY`; `BB` = `BLOCKED_BY_BS16`; `NI` = `NOT_IMPLEMENTED`; `N/A` = `NOT_APPLICABLE`. Expanded definitions follow the table.

| Physical operation | Governance | Budget | Reservation | Obligation | Recognition | Payable | Settlement | Ledger |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Maintenance | PP | NA | NA | NA | PP | NA | BB | RC |
| Repair | PP | NA | NA | NA | PP | NA | BB | RC |
| Replacement | PP | NA | NA | PP | PP | NA | BB | RC |
| Renovation / refurbishment | PP | NA | NA | NA | PP | NA | BB | RC |
| Construction / new project | PP | NA | NA | PP | PP | NA | BB | RC |
| Cancellation / withdrawal | PP | NA | NA | PP | PP | NA | BB | RC |
| Recurring Facility OPEX | PP | NA | NA | NI | PP | NA | BB | RC |

Cell interpretations:

- **Governance (`PP`):** no canonical facility-spend approval decision/grant or execution gate exists. Existing Governance structures are reusable as authority infrastructure only after a product decision maps a real decision type, actor/body, rights, rejection and execution rule.
- **Budget (`NA`):** Finance has canonical category/period/headroom projections. No approved Facility cost source/category mapping currently supplies the correct amount and action, and CFI7 is not an advance budget gate.
- **Reservation (`NA`):** Finance reservation facts are not used by CFI7 for the physical operation. Decide when a budget/liquidity claim is reserved and when it may be consumed or released.
- **Obligation (`NA`/`PP`):** CFI7 can create canonical commitments for explicit caller-supplied facts on narrow paths. Which operation/approval creates a binding obligation, and when, is unapproved; no project name or physical start can stand in for it.
- **Recognition (`PP`):** Finance primitives exist and CFI7 can create recognition for supported facts; OPEX/CAPEX classification and recognition point are unresolved.
- **Payable (`NA`):** canonical Finance Payables exist, but CFI7 expenditure facts are not payable-linked. A source/contract/obligation adapter is required.
- **Settlement (`BB`):** Treasury is canonical and its batch behavior is certified, but BS16 global is incomplete and the BS17 base lacks BS16L ancestry. Do not wire a temporary local settlement.
- **Ledger (`RC`):** use only canonical Finance accounts, currency, transaction and reconciliation. Never duplicate ledger state in Facilities.
- **Recurring OPEX (`NI`):** no Facility recurring-cost source or Facility-selected recurrence contract exists. If source facts are later approved, adapt to Finance's existing processor, not a Facilities scheduler.

This table classifies technical seams; it does not mean that any entry is approved to spend. Every money-changing operation remains blocked until BS16 completion and the relevant product/Governance policies are settled.

## E-P01–E-P10 unresolved policy matrix

| Policy | Existing fact / reusable rule | Decision still needed | Safe proposal, alternatives and consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| E-P01 OPEX/CAPEX | Finance categories, commitments and recognitions exist. | Classification by action and asset outcome; no approved depreciation/tax/lifetime rule. | Approve an explicit operation-to-category map, or require an operation-specific decision. If unresolved, do not price/post. |
| E-P02 Cost sources | CFI7 accepts explicit amount/currency input; Finance prevents implicit money creation. | Authoritative quote, contract, source data, or manager-entered amount and validation rights. | Use a sourced quote/contract or explicitly authorized input; alternative is unknown cost and no start. Never formula-price from quality/capacity. |
| E-P03 Commitment time | Finance separates reservation, commitment, recognition and payment. | Exact event that reserves capacity and creates the binding obligation. | Reserve before irreversible contracting only if policy says so; otherwise another explicit lifecycle point. Need alone/proposal must never bind funds. |
| E-P04 Governance | Governance rights are dated and command-specific. | Proposal/approval/rejection/veto actors and affected decision type. | Reuse one canonical decision authority with explicit grants. Alternative is no spending command until authority exists; no arbitrary thresholds. |
| E-P05 Execution lifecycle | Facilities work and Finance events have independent owners. | Contract point, physical start, completion and accounting trigger. | Persist cross-references at each approved event; physical progress never implies recognition/payment. |
| E-P06 Cancellation | Finance facts are append-only and settlement is separately recorded. | What may be released, reduced, credited or refunded after each stage. | Preserve sunk/recognized/payable facts; record compensating facts only when a valid refund/credit source exists. |
| E-P07 Recurrence | Finance source engine/calendar can process explicit schedules. | Facility source kind, amount, currency, recurrence, end/cancel source. | Emit only from an approved service contract/source; alternative is no recurrent cost. No automatic utility or upkeep assumption. |
| E-P08 Insufficient funds | Finance reports authorization/headroom and liquidity separately; no automatic debt. | Which gate blocks proposal, commitment or physical start, and handling after a valid contract exists. | Surface exact Finance reason and stop at a policy-defined boundary; alternative is keep obligation due while pausing work, if legally approved. |
| E-P09 Currency/period | Finance resolves explicit currency, account and fiscal period; no FX conversion. | Missing/unsupported currency and period rules for each sourced cost. | Require explicit valid currency and Finance account mapping; unsupported/mixed currency blocks rather than converts. |
| E-P10 Traceability | CFI7 `FacilityFinancialBinding` links physical source IDs to Finance artifact IDs. | Required links for reservation, governance decision, purchase/contract, payable and settlement. | Extend only the cross-reference binding required by the approved lifecycle; Finance collections stay canonical. |

All proposals above are `PROPOSAL`, not `DECIDED`. No Finance or Governance authority has been changed.

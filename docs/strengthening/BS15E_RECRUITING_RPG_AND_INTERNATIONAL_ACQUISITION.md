# BS15E · Recruiting RPG and International Acquisition

## Authority

`Player` / `Person` remain the only identity. `Player.basketball.ratings` is player truth and is not read to build the recruiting board, relationship state, or choice projection. Public class tier/rank is generated on an independent deterministic stream. Recruiting preferences live in `RecruitProfile.recruitingRpg.preferenceProfile`; they are human motivation truth, separate from basketball ability. The profile remains attached to the existing `PlayerId` and is optional for legacy V4 saves.

Each program has its own `RecruitingIntel` belief bands and confidence. Contact, conversation and visit actions update only the acting program's beliefs. Exact preference values and stakeholder opinion are not shown in the workspace. Program evaluation still uses `OrganizationKnowledge` through the existing valuation API.

`RecruitingRelationship` keeps familiarity, rapport, trust and credibility separately for the head coach, current primary recruiter and program. Contact/pitch actions are owned by the recruiter; campus visits involve the head coach. Relationship history is retained if an assistant leaves, while the choice projection counts rapport only for the currently assigned recruiter. Stakeholders carry a bounded influence and a preference; their attitude can shift a decision but does not choose for the Player.

## Interactions, context and decisions

Contact, pitch and visit consume recruiting capacity. Same-day repeated contact reduces response and trust. A visit uses actual roster counts at the prospect's position and relationship trust; it can improve or damage the program signal. Promises are recorded at their strength and position, and contradictory same-position assurances leave an explicit story event. Promises are not cash and do not create NIL, aid or scholarship authority.

Offers remain separate from commitment and signing. Commitment choice is a deterministic comparison of multidimensional preference, real position opportunity, current coach relationship, recorded role assurances, stakeholder influence, decision style, visits and a small recruiting signal. It returns qualitative reasons for tests and is not displayed as a universal probability or an authoritative interest score. Verbal commitments may change while unsigned if the competitive picture changes. AI uses the same contact/offer gateways and ranks targets through organization knowledge, not hidden Player ratings or private rival state.

The current negotiation engine can open a program-specific negotiation from modeled priorities, record prospect concerns/requests, and apply factual explanations, promises, refusals, redirects, delays or pressure. These operations update trust, credibility, promise state, unresolved topics and story history. The store and Recruiting workspace expose these actions. Verbal commitment transitions active negotiations to terminal committed/rejected states. AI now opens negotiations from its program-specific RecruitingIntel and uses the shared response operation; epistemic-parity and controlled three-program behavior are not yet certified.

The visit action now records official/unofficial type, dates, lodging nights, participants, source action and legal status. NCAA official visits check the current verified one-before/one-on-or-after October 15 institutional count and two-night lodging limit using calendar configuration; missing lodging rules fail closed. The workspace still launches an unofficial same-day visit only, and official-visit academic documentation, head-coach-departure exceptions, and full manual user validation remain open. The shared permission gate distinguishes recruiting/contact, evaluation, quiet, dead and shutdown periods; applies configured evaluation-event filters; applies communication start dates, WBB July blackout, class/location off-campus rules, signed-prospect restrictions, seven annual opportunities and selected July visit restrictions. Approved live evaluations can be recorded in the same history ledger, which derives opportunity usage and configured off-campus person-days. Optional calendar data remains supported for old saves and non-NCAA fixtures; NCAA actions fail closed when the required calendar is absent. Successor seasons use clearly labeled simulated carry-forward data until a newer verified source is added. See the NCAA rules research note for supported periods and known limits.

Formal signing-window formulas and signing-day contact restrictions are now enforced at the shared permission gate, and signing remains separate from verbal commitment. The regular opening resolver reads the completed `FINAL` game date from the source season's competition format; its end date comes only from a per-program `RecruitingInstitutionalSigningPolicy`. Cycles carry that policy forward with `SIMULATED_CARRY_FORWARD` provenance. Missing final-game or institutional policy data blocks regular-period signing. The engine does not yet provide a user-facing policy configuration workflow. Official visit count/lodging checks and the canonical Staff action ledger are present, but complete workload coverage across every action is not certified. AI response selection has a focused hidden-preference invariance test; AI operation parity and the full gameplay fairness matrix remain open.

## International and eligibility handoff

International prospects use the same `RecruitProfile` and canonical `PlayerId`; international concerns are represented as preference dimensions rather than a flat international penalty. `materializeRecruitingTalentCandidate` materializes a BS15B cohort candidate via `RECRUITING_POOL` and creates a profile around that same PlayerId, idempotently. `discoverRecruitingTalentCandidate` exposes a bounded, deterministic, one-candidate intake route to both the Recruiting workspace and AI; it does not reveal hidden ratings or priorities. Focused integration coverage follows one cohort candidate through discovery, Scouting/OrganizationKnowledge, Recruiting, signing and BS15D arrival while asserting stable PlayerId/PersonId. The required three-program international decision scenario remains open.

BS15D remains the only NCAA enrollment authority. Signed college arrivals are staged through the existing roster prerequisite, passed to `enrollPlayer`, assessed with the active `CollegeRuleset`, and retained on the roster only if eligible. A failed enrollment/assessment blocks arrival with an explanation in recruiting history. Enrollment, eligibility and roster membership remain separate authorities; no UI writes roster state. The route still does not model decision-time eligibility uncertainty in depth.

## Save and future handoff

The expanded `RecruitProfile` is already part of the V1–V4 compatibility payload, so preferences, organization intel, relationships, stakeholders, promises and concise story history round-trip without a new save version. If no RPG payload exists, deterministic defaults are materialized on the first interaction. BS15F owns portal transitions and actual NIL/economic arrangements; downstream promise fulfillment and retention consequences remain future work.

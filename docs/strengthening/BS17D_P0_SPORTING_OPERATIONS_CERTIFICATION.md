## Production chain

The shared `facilitiesSporting/SportingOperationResolver` consumes CFI anatomy/capability helpers, CFI2 purpose-agnostic Team rights and BS17C physical availability/work restrictions. It returns the five approved statuses and identifiable candidate-resource evidence without computing sporting effects. Alternatives are evaluated in sorted ComponentId order; an available, compatible entitled alternative can restore operation despite another unavailable component.

`TrainingPhysicalRequirements` is the explicit v1 operation registry. It covers identifiable shooting, finishing, ball-handling, passing, physical defense/rebounding drills and on-court tactical systems. Tactical court work checks known full-court geometry. Practice and full basketball courts use the existing anatomy capability families, not a duplicated type catalog. Cognitive work, generic physical conditioning, team cohesion, recovery and rest receive no invented specialized resource requirement. No current catalog module specifies video or an instrumental strength/cardio modality; the implementation does not infer equipment from a target rating. User modules inherit their canonical base definition; delegated sessions check the actual effective definition.

The real scheduled-session executor resolves that effective operation before applying any effects. VERIFIED_UNAVAILABLE leaves the same session pending and records the failed attempt, with no stimulus, fatigue, morale, cohesion, injury or delegation-outcome mutation. VERIFIED_AVAILABLE, compatible VERIFIED_LIMITED and UNVERIFIED_PHYSICAL_CONTEXT execute through the unchanged Training formulas and participation/Staff authorities. Completion remains immutable and idempotent. No second training agenda exists.

The manager sees historical attempt status, operation/date, Facility/Component/capability, access/physical reason, nominal capacity and unknown operating capacity in canonical Training. Blocked work links to Facilities and can be explicitly rescheduled for tomorrow through the existing validated scheduling command; schedule/Staff conflicts remain enforced. The previous failed attempt survives rescheduling until a new attempt replaces it. Same-day restoration permits retry directly through the same executor. There is no automatic delayed execution policy.

## Unknown, access and capacity

No component evidence preserves legacy continuity with UNVERIFIED_PHYSICAL_CONTEXT; UNKNOWN is never a fabricated zero or perfect condition. A known candidate without active scoped Team rights is VERIFIED_UNAVAILABLE. Organization-only rights, ownership, control and operation do not grant Team use. Unknown detail cannot authorize a denied known candidate. A legitimately accessible alternative with unknown condition permits explicitly unverified continuity.

LIMITED is not a multiplier. Existing usable capabilities are preserved unless concrete known geometry or physical work makes the requested operation incompatible. Unknown geometry needed by an operation remains unverified. OUT_OF_SERVICE and BS17C scoped work prevent use of the affected resource.

Capacity evidence retains known amount/unit; operating capacity remains null. Neither demand nor an interval creates a reservation. No agenda currently supplies an authorized shared allocator, so quantitative capacity enforcement is not claimed, including when nominal units are comparable. Shared Team rights are honored independently of capacity reservation.

## Medical and Recovery boundaries

Inspected production authorities: MedicalAdvisory/MedicalRiskAssessment, Rehabilitation, FitnessTest, ReturnToPlayEngine, MedicalActionAccess and the AI lifecycle. They contain clinical advice, plan choice, bounded setbacks, tests and clearance. They do not contain imaging, instrumental physiotherapy, hydrotherapy/cryotherapy or an explicitly equipped specialized rehabilitation procedure to which the approved material restriction can legitimately attach.

Creating such procedures is expressly forbidden by P0. Their Facilities consequence is therefore NOT_IMPLEMENTED, not another unresolved generic infrastructure-policy decision. Clinical review and existing fitness evaluation do not acquire a clinic/imaging requirement. Negative paired-world tests demonstrate equal fitness results, setbacks, RTP decisions, resting-session effects and daily fatigue recovery across FULL/OOS infrastructure. No injury duration, severity, recurrence risk, recommendation, fatigue formula or automatic clearance is modified.

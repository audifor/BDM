Audit base: `c23771ff51146ff7ce7a738504d54c443438b3ca`. `GameWorld` is the normalized persistent state boundary. Domain factories and `updateGameWorld` validate updates; readers and projections should not become alternate authorities.

## High-risk duplicate authority findings

1. **Roster membership versus active contract team.** `Team.rosterPlayerIds` is canonical for roster membership and can contain no duplicate Player across teams. Each active `PlayerContract` separately names a `teamId`. `GameWorld.validateWorld` verifies the contract's Player and Team references, but the inspected contract-validation loop does not assert that the contract Team is the Player's roster Team. Trades and signings must keep both records aligned; malformed but referentially valid drift can therefore pass the visible invariant boundary.
2. **Free-agent classification combines two authorities.** `isPlayerFreeAgent` checks roster absence and active/scheduled contracts. This is a valid composite rule, but it means inconsistent roster/contract state changes market eligibility without a single reconciled membership transition.

## Deliberately not marked duplicate authority

- Competition participant membership versus a Season participant snapshot: these are institution-current and edition-historical state, respectively.
- Player truth versus PlayerKnowledge/OrganizationKnowledge: truth belongs to the Player; observer knowledge is intentionally uncertain and perspective-specific.
- `Game.result` versus MatchSession events/stats: completed score is persisted truth; the session/timeline/stat log are separate transient or historical records.
- Facilities status versus component condition: facility lifecycle and component serviceability are different facts.
- Coach/Team finance, Club Finance V2, Salary Cap and legacy Team salary budget: overlapping consumers exist, but these models represent personal finances, club cash flows, legal payroll, and configured salary budget respectively. The risk is weak coordination at market decisions, not proven duplicate storage of the same fact.

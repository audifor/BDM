## Ownership rule

The current persisted human identity for career orchestration is `GameWorld.userCoachId`. A state is user-owned only when its canonical record can be tied to that coach (or the coached team) through subsystem fields or Governance authority/appointment records. Inbox priority, a generic proposal status, a staff actor's offer, or a body with no user appointment is not sufficient evidence of a user decision.

## Evidence-backed gaps

- Trade proposals are transient command inputs; only completed trade records persist.
- Market negotiations persist. A user-owned `COUNTERED` offer is attributable and visible as `IMPORTANT`, but the current application has no response command or negotiation-detail surface. A future Market milestone may promote it to `ACTION_REQUIRED` after it supplies a resolver.
- No durable free-agent counteroffer/response object was found.
- Recruiting responses, training sessions, scouting work, injury timelines and eligibility terms are owned by their respective automatic lifecycles; no user response deadline is recorded for them.
- Governance can represent decisions, approvals and addressed requests. Direct requests to the user coach and approvals for a body where the user coach has an active appointment are attributable and visible as `IMPORTANT`; a future Governance milestone may promote them to `ACTION_REQUIRED` after it supplies a resolver.

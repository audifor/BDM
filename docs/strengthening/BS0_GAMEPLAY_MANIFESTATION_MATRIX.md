## Reading the matrix

`COMPLETE` is scoped to the named dimension and existing supported behavior. It does not claim the whole subsystem is complete. In particular, MatchEngine is COMPLETE for deterministic match execution, but not for persistent fatigue, finance feedback, or complete game-loop consequences. Finance V2 is COMPLETE for canonical persistence/reporting, not for daily gameplay activation. Facilities is COMPLETE for its own canonical model and save persistence, not for gameplay activation.

## E-P01 · Classification by nature and evidenced scope

**Status:** `APPROVED_PRINCIPLE` · `DETAIL_PENDING`.

Classify operations by their nature and accredited scope. Maintenance and restorative repair are OPEX candidates. Structural improvements, expansion, and construction are CAPEX candidates. Replacement and mixed work depend on evidenced scope and a legitimate breakdown. No additional accounting treatment is approved. The precise Finance category mapping and accepted breakdown evidence remain pending.

## E-P02 · Hybrid cost model

**Status:** `APPROVED_PRINCIPLE` · `DETAIL_PENDING`.

Use documented costs and simulated offers produced only through a canonical approved economic model. When adequate sources are missing, report `UNKNOWN_COST`. This ratification generates no prices and does not approve a pricing mechanism.

### E-P02-SIM · Pending sub-contract

Define approved sources and provenance, reference values, calibration, model versions, offer identity and generation, acceptance, and validity. Specify missing-source handling and how documented costs coexist with simulated offers. No values, formulas, or mechanism are approved until this sub-contract is decided.

## E-P04 · Three-level Governance

**Status:** `APPROVED_PRINCIPLE` · `DETAIL_PENDING`.

Use operational, institutional, and strategic authority levels. Resolve all permissions through explicit, effective, traceable grants. This does not assign any grant, give the manager universal authority, or create a parallel Board. Existing Governance types, events, rights, appointments, and grants remain the authority.

### E-P04-GOV-BOOTSTRAP · Pending sub-contract

Define how simulated organizations obtain source-backed institutions, bodies/appointments, and effective grants; document grant provenance, dates, and behavior when a grant is absent or unknown. No grants may be initialized by assumption before this policy is approved.

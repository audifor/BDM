## Correctly inactive or superseded systems

- Legacy 35-rating / overall model is superseded by the Player Truth 80-rating model. Do not reactivate old authority.
- Early MatchEngine prototype is superseded by MatchEngine V3. Match Next is a separate branch/kernel and research source at the reviewed HEAD, not authority to replace V3.
- Legacy Team salary budget and modern Club Finance, Salary Cap payroll, and Coach personal finance have distinct meanings. Existing affordability use is a coordination risk; blindly activating every balance as one “budget” would create a new conflict.
- PCB-local facilities values are not a canonical mirror of Facilities V2. Keep old UI as reference only until it is bound to the current domain.

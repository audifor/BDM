2. Governance pending work: make the existing decision records visible and decide which existing `IMPORTANT` candidates should stop time or route to an action.
3. Club Finance: show actual ledger/commitment/forecast facts, then bind recommendations to approved actions and calendar checkpoints.
4. Contract planning: show BS11A evidence adjacent to an explicit review workflow, with renewal policy taken from the approved BS11B/BS11C decisions.
5. Player and Staff dynamics: present existing evidence with its meaning and confidence; connect only signal consumers that have an approved authority.

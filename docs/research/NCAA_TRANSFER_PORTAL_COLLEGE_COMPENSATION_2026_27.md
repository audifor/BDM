# NCAA transfer, benefits, and eligibility authority for BS15F

Reviewed 2026-10-04. The links below identify official source authority. BDM models these basketball cases and labels future fictional values as simulated; this is not a claim of universal NCAA compliance.

## Transfer Portal

- [Current Division I Manual, Article 13](https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008): the basketball notification period is 15 consecutive days starting after the championship final. BDM derives the final from its completed Competition Game. Written notice, education module and institutional processing are separate states; processing has a two-business-day deadline.
- The same Manual describes qualifying Head Coach change and athletics-aid exceptions. BDM derives coach departure/hire from canonical career history and qualifying aid reduction, cancellation or nonrenewal from AthleticsAidAgreement history. Athletic-ability changes do not qualify.
- [Adopted ghost-transfer rule](https://www.ncaa.org/news/media-center-di-cabinet-finalizes-process-for-ghost-transfer-violations/): prohibited destination aid, settlement benefits, roster submission or athletic activity can trigger the automatic Head Coach 50% championship-contest-equivalent restriction and 20% most recent sport-budget fine. BDM uses one authorization guard, canonical Enforcement/Staff restrictions, a Team-dimension Finance budget and a Finance fine. Ordinary recruiting remains blocked without Portal authorization.
- Withdrawal is a BDM product action that keeps the Player on the source roster and ends the active entry. BDM does not connect to the NCAA national database.

## Benefits and eligibility

- [Settlement benefits rules](https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/search/proposalView?id=108870) require a written institutional agreement, annual cap accounting and reporting. BDM keeps institutional benefits distinct from athletics aid, third-party NIL and PlayerContract; the signed obligation creates a canonical Finance event, commitment, recognition, payable and ledger transaction. Payment is a separate later state.
- [First-year settlement framework](https://www.ncaa.org/news/media-center-di-board-of-directors-conditionally-approves-house-settlement-related-rules-changes/) stated an approximately $20.5 million 2025–26 ceiling. BDM seeds this as the first-year source baseline in its fictional NCAA worlds. Later years carry that configured amount with `SIMULATED_CARRY_FORWARD` provenance and can be replaced by specific future configurations; they are not asserted official values.
- [Benefits cap accounting amendment](https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/search/proposalView?id=109536) excludes verified incremental athletics-scholarship overages from next-year carry; ordinary overages reduce the following cap. BDM stores exception provenance and closes/rolls caps on July 1.
- [Division I age model](https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/search/proposalView?id=109394), [NCAA clarification](https://www.ncaa.org/news/division-i-adopts-age-based-eligibility-model/): BDM stores versioned five-year clock and transition policy on CollegeRuleset, optional full-time term/class evidence and academic level on PlayerEnrollment, and explains which trigger and selection applied. Missing old evidence remains unknown. September–December 19th birthdays use the subsequent academic year unless earlier qualifying collegiate enrollment starts the clock.
- Midyear undergraduate four-year transfers may be enrolled while competition eligibility waits until the ensuing academic year. The postgraduate path is assessed separately. Destination Enrollment and BS15D availability remain the competition authority.
- [Basketball contest-limit proposal](https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/search/proposalView?id=108525) provides the 32-contest championship-segment maximum used by the current Enforcement ruleset. The ghost suspension persists a 16-contest requirement and progresses from completed contests rather than calendar days.

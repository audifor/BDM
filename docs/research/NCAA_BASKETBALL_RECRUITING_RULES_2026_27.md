## Calendar periods and differences

The men's calendar uses **recruiting, evaluation, quiet and dead periods**. It labels a recruiting period as permitting authorized staff to make off-campus recruiting contacts and evaluations; evaluation periods permit off-campus assessment but no in-person off-campus contact; quiet periods permit in-person contact only on campus; dead periods prohibit in-person contact/evaluation and campus visits. Its footnotes limit eligible events and identify specific event windows, so a period label alone is insufficient to authorize every evaluation.

The women's calendar uses **contact, evaluation, quiet, dead and recruiting-shutdown periods**. A shutdown bars all forms of recruiting (including correspondence and calls); evaluation periods permit assessment but not in-person off-campus contact; quiet periods limit in-person contact to campus; contact periods permit authorized off-campus contact and evaluations. The published 2026â€“27 chart also distinguishes prospect populations and approved scholastic/certified events in its footnotes.

Women-specific published restrictions include a July visit restriction during evaluation periods, subject to the admission/aid/deposit exceptions shown on the calendar. The men's PDF separately states a July unofficial-visit restriction subject to its admission/aid/deposit exception. These restrictions differ in text and by prospect context; they must not be collapsed into a single global rule.

The NCAA calendars are one-page visual charts. Exact period dates, sport-specific permitted activities, prospect classes, division, visit type and event approval must be read together from the calendar and the applicable current bylaw. The PDFs alone do not establish all communication start dates, staff-person-day accounting, opportunity accounting, visit timing/count limits, signing formulas or detailed indirect-contact rules. Those items require current bylaw verification before they are enabled as official-source behavior.

The MBB source PDF marks the spring dead period April 1-8, 2027; BDM uses those published dates. A newer NCAA publication should replace the template only after source verification and explicit registration. Simulated continuity must not be relabeled official.

**Official visit duration correction:** the 36-hour basketball official visit proposal was defeated (LSDBi Proposal 2021-23). Current authority is a maximum of two consecutive nights of lodging in conjunction with an official visit (Bylaw 13.6.4). BDM enforces the configured night count; it does not simulate hotel logistics or a 36-hour duration limit.

**Regular basketball signing date:** Bylaw 13.02.13.1(b) sets the initial date as Wednesday at 7 a.m. in April, one week after the NCAA men's and women's championships. The final signing date follows institutional policy for awarding scholarships/athletics aid; there is no universal NCAA end date. Figure 13-2 reports April 15, 2026 as the 2025-26 initial date, with an institutional-policy final date. This is validation data only. BDM resolves the championship from the season's completed FINAL game record and keeps the final aid date in the per-program policy record. Future carry-forward cycles retain that policy with simulated provenance; missing anchor or policy blocks the regular period.

## BDM implementation boundary and season continuity

`NCAA_DI_MBB_2026_27` and `NCAA_DI_WBB_2026_27` are the initial source-backed Recruiting rules authority, effective for 2026-27 and grounded in the NCAA calendar sources above. A source-backed ruleset is not an eternal rule for later NCAA seasons.

Every initialized NCAA `RecruitingCycle` receives the built-in category-specific template or the latest valid prior cycle template. If no newer verified external ruleset has been added, later cycles receive a versioned `BDM-CONTINUITY-<season>-v1` ruleset derived from that authority. Provenance is `SIMULATED_CARRY_FORWARD`; source season, derived season and source ruleset are retained, and the source explicitly says the result is not official NCAA data. There is no unrestricted all-cycle `CONTACT` fallback for NCAA production cycles. Missing calendars fail closed in the permission query. The fixed-date baseline can derive distant seasons such as 2045-46, but those remain BDM simulations.

The calendar gate remains a partial simulation model, not a complete NCAA compliance engine. Opportunity accounting derives countable contacts/evaluations from persisted action history; off-campus evaluation records derive person-day use, subject to configured exceptions. Official visit history retains count and lodging inputs. Regular signing resolves both external authorities and fails closed when required data is absent. Full official visit prerequisites/head-coach departure exceptions, user-facing institutional signing-policy setup, season-scoped Staff designation/workload lifecycle, negative-recruiting Governance integration, information-boundary proof and end-to-end international AI parity remain incomplete. Final college enrollment remains under the dated BS15D `CollegeRuleset`/`PlayerEnrollment` authority. BDM implements only the verified Division I basketball rules configured for its 2026-27 ruleset; carry-forward calendars are simulation data and are not proof of NCAA compliance.
